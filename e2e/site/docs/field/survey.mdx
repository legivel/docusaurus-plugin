A survey walks the transect at dawn. The logger prints the codes listed in [Status codes](../reference/codes.md). Uploads follow [RFC 9110](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9110).

## Pick the route

<Tabs>
  <TabItem value="summer" label="Summer route">Start at the north gate and walk the ridge.</TabItem>
  <TabItem value="winter" label="Winter route">Start at the boathouse and keep to the shore path.</TabItem>
</Tabs>

:::warning Check the radio
Test the radio at the gate before you walk out.
:::

```bash title="survey-start.sh"
kestrel-log start --transect north --interval 30s
```
